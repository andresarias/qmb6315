QMB 6315 — Interactive Statistics

Business Analysis for Decision Making · FIU · Fall 2026
Work through each one before that week’s homework. Nothing to install, nothing to submit.

Week 2
How unusual is one number?
The normal distribution, z-scores, and the one question people forget to ask: is this data actually shaped like a bell?
Week 3
Where do sample means come from?
Draw samples from a lopsided population and watch their averages assemble themselves into a bell curve. The central limit theorem, done by hand.
Week 4
What does “95% confident” actually mean?
Run a hundred clinical trials. Watch about five of them miss the truth entirely, through no fault of their own.
Weeks 5–6
Is this drug doing anything?
Hypotheses, p-values, and the two questions people mistake for one: is the effect real, and is it big enough to care about?
All three follow OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e.
Best on a laptop. They work on a phone, but you will scroll.
