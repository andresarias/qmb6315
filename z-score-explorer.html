The normal distribution is a model, not a law

A z-score can be computed for any number in any dataset. It is just subtraction and division. That is not the question.

The question is whether you are then allowed to look that z-score up on a normal table and call the answer a percentile. You are only allowed to do that if the data is actually shaped like a bell. Switch between the two datasets above and watch the two percentages come apart.

This matters far beyond this course. Most real business data — costs, incomes, claim sizes, time-to-completion, revenue per customer — is right-skewed like the charges. Running it through a normal model produces confident, precise, wrong answers.
