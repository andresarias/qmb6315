What least squares will do to you

Least squares squares the misses. A person the line misses by 60 contributes 3,600 to the total; a person it misses by 6 contributes 36. One hundred times the penalty for ten times the miss. The line will therefore bend a long way to avoid one extreme point, at the cost of fitting everyone else slightly worse.

Press Add one unusual patient and watch. That single person is older than anyone else in the study and has cholesterol far below the trend — and because they sit at the far edge of the age range, they have leverage: the line pivots around the middle of the data, so a point at the end moves it most.

Nothing in the arithmetic will warn you. The formula returns a slope, an r2, and a confident-looking equation either way. The only defence is looking at the scatter plot first — which is why the book has you draw one before it lets you fit anything.
